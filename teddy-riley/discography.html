Teddy Riley Discography and Production Credits

A record of the work behind the sound.

Teddy Riley’s work runs through the story of modern R&B and hip-hop: early Harlem recordings, the birth of New Jack Swing, Guy and BLACKstreet, landmark productions for Bobby Brown, Keith Sweat, Michael Jackson and many more, plus a continuing run of collaborations around the world.

This independent archive is designed to make that body of work easier to explore. Each release is catalogued as a work rather than every regional pressing: the relevant songs, versions, label information, sleeve art and, where available, credited personnel. The collection is continually checked against original releases and published credit sources.

What this discography documents

The archive focuses on work credited to Teddy Riley as a producer, writer, remixer, performer or collaborator. It preserves version-specific details—instrumentals, radio edits, bonus beats, dub mixes and other variations—that can disappear from standard streaming discographies.

Credits are retained as research data and cross-checked against physical releases and available published sources. If you can correct a release or identify a missing version, get in touch with the archive.

Explore the archive

Follow the Teddy Riley timeline for the story around the releases, or use the site search to find a song, artist, label, producer, engineer or writer across the collection. The archive keeps its research record open: releases with an uncollected lossless version are clearly marked, and the Missing list records the versions still being sought.

TR Productions is an independent research archive and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Teddy Riley.
